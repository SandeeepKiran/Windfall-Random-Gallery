Windfall Gallery deals your Android photo library at random, so the photos you forgot about come back. Swipe for a new random page, heart the ones you love so they show up more often, or play up to six videos at once. It has no internet permission at all. Free and open source, and new in 1.5.0: tablets, Chromebooks, keyboard and mouse. github.com/SandeeepKiran/Windfall-Random-Gallery
